Room 401

Shift Survivors

07:00 to 19:00. Nobody gets left behind.

Survive a 12-hour shift at St. Overtime Medical Center. Answer call lights, run neuro checks, catch stroke alerts, discharge and admit, and grow your team until 19:00.

  • Real nursing work at arcade speed
  • In 3D: dress your nurse in the Locker
  • Build your team: RNs, CNAs and a resource nurse
  • Level up to run more units: up to 4 floors and 168 beds
  • Perks, badge reels and 14 achievements
  • In English and Spanish
  • Keyboard, controller and Steam Deck

About

Made by a nurse

Every Loyal4 game starts with real nursing work: call lights, neuro checks, admissions, the coffee run. The goal is games that play fast and still feel true to the floor.

Shift Survivors started on a hospital floor as something for staff to play on break, and grew into the studio's first game.
